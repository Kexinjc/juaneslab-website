      title: Research
      subtitle: Cytoskeletal dynamics in cell migration and cancer invasion
      text: |
        The Juanes Lab studies how the cytoskeleton is remodeled during collective cell migration and cancer invasion — combining techniques from single-molecule biochemistry to advanced live-cell and super-resolution imaging, in models ranging from reconstituted proteins to mice. The lab uses function-separation mutants and a multi-scale approach, drawing on biochemistry, genetics, proteomics and cell biology alongside quantitative live imaging.

        **Research objectives**

        - Understanding the mechanisms underlying cytoskeleton remodeling events in collective cell migration, cancer invasion and metabolism.
        - Identifying novel genetic signatures and/or biomarkers for the early detection of human cancers, to ultimately improve cancer diagnosis and assist in personalized therapy.
