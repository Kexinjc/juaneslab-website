        <p class="jl-project-sponsor">PREP2022-000642 — Ministerio de Ciencia, Innovación y Universidades / Agencia Estatal de Investigación (MICIU/AEI) and FSE+</p>

        This four-year predoctoral fellowship supports Irene Olivas Cano's PhD research at the Juanes Lab. Her work investigates actin-based protrusions and their contribution to cancer cell migration and invasion.

        ### FLIP-ACTIN: Flipping the coin — 'Actin' for good
        <p class="jl-project-sponsor">PID2022-140899NB-I00 — Ministerio de Ciencia, Innovación y Universidades / Agencia Estatal de Investigación (MICIU/AEI)</p>

        FLIP-ACTIN investigates the mechanisms that drive cancer cell migration and invasion, with a particular focus on actin-based protrusions and their relationship with cellular metabolism. The project also supports research personnel and predoctoral training in the lab.

        ### Intramural fellowships
        <p class="jl-project-sponsor">Centro de Investigación Príncipe Felipe (CIPF)</p>

        CIPF intramural fellowships support the professional development and specialist training of Juanes Lab members, including external workshops and training placements at institutions such as Institut Curie in Paris and the biotechnology company ProtoQSAR.

        <hr class="jl-project-sep" />

        <div class="jl-project-row">
        <div class="jl-project-copy">

        ### Cytoskeletal coordination mediated by APC in collective cell migration
        <p class="jl-project-sponsor">CIDEGENT2021/026 — Generalitat Valenciana</p>

        Awarded through the Generalitat Valenciana programme for attracting and returning research talent, this funding enabled Dr María Ángeles Juanes to return to Spain and establish her independent research group at CIPF in 2022.

        The project investigates how the tumour suppressor APC coordinates cytoskeletal dynamics during collective cell migration.

        </div>

        <hr class="jl-project-sep" />

        ### Molecular coordination between cytoskeletal networks in cell migration and its impact on development and disease
        <p class="jl-project-sponsor">SBF006/1070 — The Academy of Medical Sciences (UK)</p>

        This Springboard Award supported Dr María Ángeles Juanes in establishing her first independent laboratory at the National Horizons Centre, Teesside University, in 2021. The research investigated the molecular coordination between cytoskeletal networks during cell migration and its implications for development and disease.

        <p class="jl-project-sponsor">Teesside University, School of Health and Life Sciences (UK)</p>
