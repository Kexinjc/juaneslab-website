Kexin studied Data Science and later completed a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence, Pattern Recognition and Digital Imaging at the Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV). She then worked as a research assistant at the Valencian Research Institute for Artificial Intelligence (VRAIN), where her research focused on the evaluation and analysis of large language models. She is currently working in Dr. Juanes's laboratory, applying artificial intelligence and computer vision methods to biological image analysis and cell tracking.

Outside of work, Kexin enjoys playing the piano and guitar, playing tennis and reading.
